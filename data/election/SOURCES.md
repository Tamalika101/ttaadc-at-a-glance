# 2026 TTAADC election data: sources and checks

Election held 12 April 2026; results declared 17 April 2026. 25 of 28 seats are reserved for Scheduled Tribes.

The official results portal (https://sec-results.tripura.gov.in/sec/) cannot be reached from outside India,
including from GitHub's servers, so the figures were compiled by hand on 14 Nov 2026 from:

- `ttaadc_2026_results.csv`: Wikipedia, "2026 Tripura Tribal Areas Autonomous District Council election",
  constituency-wise table (winner and runner-up only). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Tripura_Tribal_Areas_Autonomous_District_Council_election
- `party_totals.csv`: Voterlist.co.in results page, party-wise votes polled.
  https://voterlist.co.in/ttaadc-election-results-2026-live-counting-updates/

Cross-checks (all matched):
- Winner vote shares for all 28 seats match the Voterlist.co.in table.
- Seat 1 (12,836 vs 9,022), seat 3 (13,467 vs 8,482) and seat 4 (14,348 vs 12,543): Tripura Star News, https://www.tripurastarnews.com/?p=108624
- Seat 5 margin 1,698 and seat 22 margin 10,654: India TV live results, 17 Apr 2026.
- Seat tally 24 TMP, 4 BJP: India TV, Sentinel Assam, NewsX.

Constituency names follow the Voterlist/India TV spelling; spellings vary across sources.
Only the top two candidates are listed per seat, so other parties appear only in the party totals.
